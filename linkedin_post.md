Which customers would you pay to save? 📉

I built a churn-and-ROI decision engine on the real IBM Telco dataset:
- **26.5% churn** — 1,869 customers gone
- **~$139k/month revenue** walking out the door
- Model AUC **0.84** ranks every customer by dollar-at-risk

One insight hits hardest: churn is an **acquisition problem**, not a
loyalty problem — churners have been customers for a median of just
**10 months** (stayers: 38). And month-to-month contracts churn at
**42.7%** vs **2.8%** on two-year deals.

What the project actually delivers, not just a chart:
→ a ranked save-list + a **costed retention program with ROI**
→ every number computed and verified in Python — the AI layer only
explains, prioritizes and frames; it never invents a metric
→ an interactive dashboard you can PDF-export and walk a client through

Project 1 of 9 in a full end-to-end portfolio (consulting, ops, fintech,
marketing, AI, and more). Building in public — next up: operations analytics.
