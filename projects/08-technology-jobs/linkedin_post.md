"Data Architect" sits at the top of the AI/data pay ladder, seniority is a ~2.4x
multiplier, and where the company sits matters as much as what you do. I mapped it
from public salary data. 💼

Dataset: public ds_salaries mirror (Kaggle era 2020-2024), ~600 verified records:

→ Leveling beats title inflation: SE medians run 2.4x EN — the negotiation skill that
   matters is getting the level right
→ Geography ladder computed (n≥30 markets only — honesty filters on)
→ Remote vs onsite medians side by side — it's a menu, not a law
→ YoY median trajectory charted

Same rule as every project here: Python computes and verifies; AI only narrates the
verified numbers (with sample-bias caveats written down, not hidden).

Interactive dashboard in the repo. Useful for anyone job-hunting in data/AI right now.
