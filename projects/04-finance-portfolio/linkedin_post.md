# LinkedIn post — Project 04 (Finance)

---

The S&P 500 fell 85% and took ~15 years to fully recover. I put 150 years of market
history into one dashboard so finance committees can stop planning around vibes. 📉📈

Using the 1871→present public dataset (~1,900 months, Shiller-derived):

→ Inflation-honest returns: I split nominal vs CPI-adjusted history on a log-scale chart
→ The patience curve: forward 20-year real returns ranged from -5.4% (worst entry
   ever) to +6%+ (90th percentile entry) — timing matters less than staying in
→ Top-10 drawdowns with real recovery durations (deepest: -85%, average top-10
   recovery: ~9 years!)
→ Same math powers an AI-written CFO brief — narrating verified numbers only,
   never inventing them

Charts, code, and the interactive dashboard are in the repo (public data, fully
reproducible in 5 commands).
