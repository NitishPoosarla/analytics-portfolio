150,000 citizen complaints. One quarter. And the fastest agency closes a median case
in 1.5 hours while the slowest needs 700+. That gap is a business problem — so I
turned it into an operations deck. 🏙️

What the data says (NYC 311, public open data, Jun–Aug 2026):
→ ~1 in 4 cases still open at quarter end (backlog is concentrated, not diffuse)
→ The >72h SLA queue = 36k cases — one sweep at 35% completion is fully costed
→ Complaints arrive in a predictable evening wave — staffing should ride the curve,
   not fight it
→ Re-deploying 12% of capacity onto the 5 slowest heavy-volume complaint types frees
   ~$X00k of staff-hours per quarter (assumptions written down, not hand-waved)

Built with Python + pandas, an interactive dashboard anyone can click through,
and the AI rule I follow in every project: **AI explains verified numbers. It never
computes them.**
